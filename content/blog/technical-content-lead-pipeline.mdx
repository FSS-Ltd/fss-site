Publishing technical content is not enough. The page has to **guide the next step** for the reader.

## Match each post to one conversion intent

Every article should have one primary conversion goal:

- Book a demo
- Download a practical guide
- Request an implementation audit

When a post has too many goals, conversion quality drops.

## Build contextual CTAs inside the article body

Readers often decide before they reach your footer CTA. Add contextual CTA blocks near key decision points.

<Callout title="Editorial guideline">
Place your strongest CTA after the section that explains business impact, not only at the end.
</Callout>

## Use internal linking to improve discoverability and progression

Internal links do two jobs:

1. Help search engines understand topic depth
2. Move readers through your conversion journey

Link implementation-focused content to your [SDK integration playbook](/blog/sdk-integration-playbook) and then to `/contact`.
