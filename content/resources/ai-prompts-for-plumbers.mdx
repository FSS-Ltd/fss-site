## Draft admin messages from facts you supply

This kit gives plumbing business owners a starting point for common written tasks. It covers enquiries, job notes, quotes, booking confirmations, completion summaries, payment reminders, honest review requests and local social posts.

Use placeholders for customer details. The AI drafts text; you check the facts, prices, timing and technical wording before sending anything. The kit does not connect to your diary, send messages or verify payment records.
