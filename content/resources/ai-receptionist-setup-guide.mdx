<details>
<summary>What tools does the guide use?</summary>

The worked pilot uses Retell AI for the conversation, Cal.com for appointment rules and bookings, and Twilio for a UK telephone connection. Begin with one location, one practitioner or resource, one appointment type and one language. The PDF includes links to the primary documentation checked on 12 October 2026.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I keep my existing booking system?</summary>

Your existing booking platform should remain the authoritative appointment record. Check that a supported integration can both read availability and create the right booking. Where that is not available, start with approved FAQs and a human handover. FSS can help assess [workflow automation and integrations](/services/workflow-automation).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the whole setup free?</summary>

The guide is free. Voice usage, telephone numbers, scheduling features, messages and operational support can have separate costs. The guide explains the cost layers and includes an illustrative budget, not a quote. Verify supplier plans and prices before purchasing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is this suitable for a clinic?</summary>

The starter scope is administrative: routine information, new bookings and a route to a person. It is not clinical advice, diagnosis or triage. Begin with fictional callers and appointments. Complete the relevant clinical, privacy and vendor checks before handling real patient calls. The guide does not certify a system as compliant.

</details>
