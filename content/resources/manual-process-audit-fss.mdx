## Why this audit is useful

Most organizations know manual work is slowing them down, but not exactly where the biggest cost and risk sit. This audit gives your team a structured way to measure the impact and make evidence-based improvement decisions.

## What you get

1. A process inventory template for high-effort workflows
2. A risk-scoring method for manual task failure points
3. An automation prioritization matrix for quick-win and strategic initiatives

## How to use it

Start with one department, complete the audit with process owners, then rank opportunities by operational impact and implementation effort before building your delivery roadmap.
