## Why this framework matters

Software decisions often fail because teams optimize for features, not outcomes. This framework helps you evaluate initiatives by strategic impact, implementation complexity, and operational risk.

## What is included

1. An investment scoring model for prioritizing initiatives
2. A risk and dependency assessment guide for modernization projects
3. A practical operating cadence for executive and delivery reviews

## Recommended rollout

Start by evaluating your top three planned software initiatives, score each with the framework, and then align budget and delivery milestones based on business impact and execution confidence.
