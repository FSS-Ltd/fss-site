# Outreach video research brief

Status: source notes for a proposed design, not approved campaign copy. Retrieved 17 November 2026.

## Provenance and limits

All three English, automatically generated YouTube caption tracks were retrieved with `youtube-transcript-api` in an isolated temporary environment. Timestamps below refer to caption segment starts. Caption wording was not checked against audio. The creators' examples and revenue arithmetic are not independent evidence of conversion, churn, costs, or profitability. ## Review-gap permission offer

Source: [Review plan](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_nL25X5Ub9s).

- **02:35–05:49:** compare a well-rated prospect's recent review pace with a comparable mid-market competitor. Record the observation date, totals, newest-review date, and past-year counts; recheck before outreach. A rating alone does not prove service quality.
- **06:16–07:11:** ask permission to send a short plan. Prepare the plan after an affirmative reply. Verify owner identity; never infer it from a review alone.
- **07:32–10:37:** describe a comparison and practical review-request, reminder, and reply actions. Use a real expiry date for any time-limited offer; do not promise a review count.
- **10:58–13:07:** address objections with evidence, offer sample replies, and check once or twice the following week before stopping. Exact days are not specified.
- **13:14 onward:** request payment and authorised manager access before setup. Request reviews consistently; do not select only satisfied customers.

## Facebook group website offer

Source: [Facebook group offer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LIORDjQCafE).

- **02:47–03:37:** find active local groups and verify their public service-posting rules. The example group sizes are not minimum thresholds.
- **03:48–05:31:** offer two local businesses a free build in exchange for feedback. Ask interested people for their business name and existing website; the first two who answer both receive initial builds.
- **08:46–09:29:** share a preview with a genuine seven-day expiry; follow up after two days and once more two days later, then stop.
- **10:19–11:23:** the speaker's reference prices are $297 for the first client, $397 for the second, and $497 thereafter. The described paid service includes lead response, review requests, and chat booking; this is not proof FSS currently provides those services.
- **11:54–13:40:** payment must clear before activation. A remaining-commenter message may claim a preview exists only when it actually exists. The speaker also describes building first.

## No-site website variant

Source: [Free website offer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EJ8sQIBEwRk).

- **03:09–04:24:** invite two local businesses to request a free build for feedback, then ask their name and whether they have a website.
- **06:41–08:49:** provide a preview with a real seven-day removal date; the example separates the free build from a $297 monthly service and keeps domain ownership with the client.
- **12:44–13:45:** a permission message can offer a preview, but a missing link in a listing is not proof no website exists. Claim a completed build only after it exists.
- **16:11–16:45:** verify generated business details and service claims. Agree terms and obtain cleared payment before onboarding or changing domains.

## Application to FSS

Adapt wording into original messages for review. The quoted prices are reference examples, not FSS prices. Free-build feedback means private product feedback, not a rewarded public review. Apply the same review-request process to all eligible customers; do not filter out customers likely to give low ratings. Review counts require authorised sources; Google Maps remains a discovery reference, not a source for prohibited bulk copying. Clients retain ownership of their domains. Any free-build, review-management, texting, or booking offer needs an approved FSS offer and evidence that FSS can deliver it. Publicly visible contact details do not establish consent. No audio verification or independent outcome study was performed.
