# Competitor and SERP research for FSS (AI integration, automation and custom software, UK)

Research date: 2026-11-11. Scope (per business owner): all UK businesses, small to large, led by AI integration and automation (AI agents, assistants and receptionists, workflow and process automation, AI added to existing systems, private AI, and the custom software and integrations that support it). Charities, faith organisations and schools are a secondary sector.

Method and limits:

- Searches were run with WebSearch, then competitor pages were visited with WebFetch. "Ranks" means "appeared in the results the search tool returned for that query". These are not verified Google positions. The tool returned no People Also Ask boxes, no AI Overviews and no search volumes.
- WebFetch gives page text, so JSON-LD schema is not visible. Schema is "unverified" for every site.
- Competitor numbers (clients, savings, uptime, review counts) are self-reported and not independently verified.
- The earlier fss_seo_opportunity_map.md was treated as a hypothesis. Its charity-led framing is superseded by the owner's scope. One Beyond (https://one-beyond.com/industries/non-profits/), ChurchSuite (https://churchsuite.com) and GetApp (https://www.getapp.co.uk/alternatives/2049793/churchsuite) returned HTTP 403, so they are not profiled.

## 1. Patterns that win

1. One page per buyer problem and per vertical. AI Agency Plus runs nine vertical answering-service pages (https://aiagencyplus.com/); Scorchsoft separates AI automation, portals, dashboards and spreadsheet-to-app (https://www.scorchsoft.com/ai-capabilities/ai-automation, https://www.scorchsoft.com/features/spreadsheet-to-app); Strategies has several private AI explainers (https://www.strategies.co.uk/what-is-private-ai-hosting-and-why-it-matters/).
2. Prices on the page. Magora, SpotDev and Plinth publish figures and each appeared for cost queries (https://magora-systems.com/uk-software-development-pricing/, https://www.spotdev.co.uk/blog/custom-customer-portal-cost-uk, https://plinth.org.uk/pricing). Geeks, Akita, Strategies, &above and Scorchsoft publish none, which leaves the "AI automation cost UK" answer to Magora and listicle sites.
3. Productised entry offers: a workshop (Akita), a diagnostic (SpotDev), a discovery sprint from £3,000 (Magora), an AI opportunity report (Scorchsoft) (URLs in profiles).
4. Dated freshness and cadence. Magora "Updated: 13 October 2026"; CloudSwitched about one post a day; AI Agency Plus three posts in two days (URLs in profiles). Pages with no dates (Geeks, Scorchsoft, Akita) are hard for readers and answer engines to trust.
5. Long guide plus table plus FAQ: about 2,000 to 3,000 words, at least one table, 4 to 9 FAQs (https://magora-systems.com/uk-software-development-pricing/, https://www.andabove.com/services/ai-agent-development, https://www.plinth.org.uk/complete-guide/software-faith-charities).
6. Named proof with numbers: &above (Tesco), Geeks (Mortgagez 60%), Scorchsoft (reviews). Firms with no proof on the page (Akita, CloudSwitched, Strategies page) look thin. All figures self-reported.
7. Low-friction enquiry: 1 to 3 required fields, a stated reply time (within the hour at SpotDev, 24 hours at CloudSwitched, one working day at Scorchsoft), and a 15 to 30 minute call (URLs in profiles).
8. Machine-readable twins: Magora llms.txt with prices; &above .md pages (https://magora-systems.com/llms.txt, https://www.andabove.com/services/ai-agent-development.md). Not checked on the rest, so unverified.
9. "Who this is not for" and build-vs-buy honesty (SpotDev non-fit list, Geeks Buy or Build Analysis, &above custom vs off-the-shelf FAQ).
