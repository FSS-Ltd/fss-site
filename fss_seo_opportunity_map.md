## D. Bottom-of-Funnel Clusters

### Service intent
This cluster matters because it captures buyers who already know they need a supplier, not just advice. Sample keywords include custom software development company UK, bespoke software development UK, dashboard development company UK, and custom portal development UK. The best page format is a strong service page with proof, process, pricing anchors, and a CTA for discovery. Lead quality is typically high because the searcher is already comparing vendors. [web:6][web:10][web:2]

### Comparison intent
This cluster matters because it intercepts buyers who are narrowing options and often have a shortlist already. Sample keywords include church suite alternatives, church management alternatives, and [competitor] vs custom software approaches. The best page format is an alternatives page or comparison guide that frames when bespoke software is a better fit. Lead quality is strong because comparison searchers are close to a decision. [web:11][web:17][web:19]

### Pricing intent
Pricing pages matter because they attract buyers who are validating budget and timing. Sample keywords include MVP development cost UK, software development cost UK, custom software pricing, and discovery phase pricing. The best page format is a cost guide with ranges, example project types, and qualification prompts. Lead quality is good if the page filters for seriousness rather than chasing price shoppers. [web:8][web:12][web:15][web:18]

### Implementation intent
This cluster matters because implementation searchers often already own a platform and need help making it work. Sample keywords include software implementation UK, portal implementation, CRM implementation, and migration/modernisation services. The best page format is a service page or migration page that explains process, risk reduction, and post-launch support. Lead quality tends to be high because implementation budgets are usually already approved. [web:3][web:10][web:23]

### Migration intent
Migration pages matter because they convert pain from outdated systems into a project. Sample keywords include legacy system migration UK, spreadsheet migration to software, and database migration services. The best page format is a migration-specific service page with risk, data handling, and phased delivery explained. Lead quality is high because migration buyers usually have urgency and operational friction. [web:10][web:3]

### Sector-specific buying intent
This cluster matters because it lets you win on context, not just technology. Sample keywords include custom software for charities UK, school parent portal development, faith charity software, and church management system alternatives. The best page format is a sector solution page with sector pain points, examples, and tailored outcomes. Lead quality is especially high when the sector has a recurring operational need and budget authority. [web:19][web:22][web:24]

### Operational pain point intent
This cluster matters because it captures the actual business problem behind the search. Sample keywords include workflow automation UK for SMEs, manual process automation, custom workflow management software, and charity impact reporting software. The best page format is a use-case page tied to measurable outcomes. Lead quality is high because these queries imply an active problem and a willingness to change systems. [web:4][web:10][web:26][web:19]

## F. Keywords to Avoid

Avoid broad informational terms like what is custom software, how to build an app, software development lifecycle, and what is MVP unless they sit inside a tightly controlled funnel. These usually attract curious readers rather than qualified buyers and tend to underperform on lead quality. [web:8][web:12][web:18]

Avoid generic “best software” terms without a sector or problem angle, because they often pull comparison traffic that is research-heavy but weak on intent. For example, best CRM, best project management tool, or best church software are often too broad unless you can target a very specific use case or buyer segment. [web:17][web:19][web:20]

Avoid high-volume platform pages that imply you are selling commodity SaaS rather than premium bespoke delivery, unless you actually plan to offer that product category. Queries such as school MIS, church management software, and donor CRM are dominated by established product vendors and are harder to convert into bespoke service leads. [web:11][web:22][web:23][web:24]
